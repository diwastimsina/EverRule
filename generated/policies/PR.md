# EverRule: prevent recurrence of INC-482 (ER-PROC-019)

## ER-PROC-019 v2

Aggregate commitments to the same vendor above $50,000 within 24 hours require a valid director approval scoped to that vendor and commitment. Missing evidence means refuse.

**Source incident:** INC-482, $78,000 unauthorized exposure
**Rule approved by:** J. Ellis, Director of Procurement Operations, 2026-09-16T00:00:00Z
**Rationale:** Split orders were not considered until the loophole check. Aggregating by vendor over 24 hours matches how the team already thinks about vendor exposure.
**Change:** `enforce()` guard in `src/procurement-policy.ts`, called from `createPurchaseOrder`. Throws `PolicyViolation` when the rule is violated or required evidence is missing.
**Tests:** 18 / 18 passed (`src/procurement-policy.test.ts`)

### Loopholes closed
- Two $40,000 orders to the same vendor, 3 hours apart
- Approval names a different vendor
- Approver is a manager, not a director
- Requester approves their own request
- Approval expired before the request
- Approval issued after the purchase order
- Same approval reused for a second order
- Approval cap is below the commitment
- EUR 70,000 with no USD conversion record

### Assumptions
- $50,000 is the existing director-approval threshold
- 24 hours is the aggregation window chosen by the business owner

### Not covered
- delegated approvals
- multi-currency totals against one vendor
- vendor identity collisions
- orders split across requesters
