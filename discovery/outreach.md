# Outreach

Under 90 words. Email or LinkedIn. Link the three-minute recording, not the URL. The URL and password go only to people who book a call. One follow-up after five business days, then stop.

## Split

Of the first 15 messages: 10 to operations owners, 5 to platform or developer-tooling leads. Keep the ratio until one count clearly leads.

## Operations owners

> **Subject:** the refund your AI agent should not have issued
>
> Hi [name], I'm building a tool that turns AI-agent mistakes (a refund or purchase order that should not have gone through) into a tested control your own systems enforce. I'm interviewing people who own [workflow] at companies running agents. 25 minutes, no pitch, and I'll share what I learn from the other interviews. Would [day/time] or [day/time] work?

| Workflow | Subject |
|---|---|
| Refunds and credits | the refund your AI agent should not have issued |
| Procurement | the purchase order your AI agent should not have created |
| Payments and disputes | the chargeback your AI agent should not have accepted |
| Account changes | the account change your AI agent should not have made |
| Access provisioning | the access your AI agent should not have granted |

Swap the example in the body to match.

## Platform and developer-tooling leads

> **Subject:** the change your coding agent should not have merged
>
> Hi [name], I'm building a tool that turns an AI-caused incident (a coding agent's change that broke production, deleted data, or bypassed a check) into a guard and a regression test, delivered as a pull request. I'm interviewing platform leads at teams using coding agents at scale. 25 minutes, no pitch, and I'll share what I learn from the other interviews. Would [day/time] or [day/time] work?

## Follow-up, day 6, both segments

> Hi [name], following up once in case this got buried. Still interviewing people who own [workflow or platform] at companies running agents. 25 minutes, no pitch. If it's not you, is there someone on your team I should ask instead?

## Rules

- Log each send in `scorecard.csv` the same day. Every reply, including a no, goes in too.
- Never attach the one-pager to a cold message. Send it after the call if asked.
- Do not quote $15,000 to a developer lead. Plan B pricing is set after five developer conversations.
