# Outreach

Under 90 words. Email or LinkedIn. One follow-up after five business days, then stop.

## Message

> **Subject:** the refund your AI agent should not have issued
>
> Hi [name], I'm building a tool that turns AI-agent mistakes (a refund or purchase order that should not have gone through) into a tested control your own systems enforce. I'm interviewing people who own [workflow] at companies running agents. 25 minutes, no pitch, and I'll share what I learn from the other interviews. Would [day/time] or [day/time] work?

## Subject lines by workflow

| Workflow | Subject |
|---|---|
| Refunds and credits | the refund your AI agent should not have issued |
| Procurement | the purchase order your AI agent should not have created |
| Payments and disputes | the chargeback your AI agent should not have accepted |
| Account changes | the account change your AI agent should not have made |
| Access provisioning | the access your AI agent should not have granted |

Swap the parenthetical example in the body to match.

## Follow-up (day 6)

> Hi [name], following up once in case this got buried. Still interviewing people who own [workflow] at companies running agents. 25 minutes, no pitch. If it's not you, is there someone on your team I should ask instead?

## Rules

- Send 15 in week 1. Log each send in `scorecard.csv` the same day.
- Every reply, including a no, goes in the scorecard.
- Never attach the one-pager to a cold message. Send it after the call if asked.
