# Pitch (locked September 29, 2026)

EverRule turns a costly AI-agent incident into a control your own systems enforce. When an agent issues a refund or creates a purchase order that violates a business rule, companies can usually see what happened, but they often don't turn that failure into a permanent control. EverRule takes the incident, drafts the rule that should have stopped it, finds loopholes in the obvious version, like two $40,000 orders that bypass a $50,000 threshold, gets the business owner to approve it, tests it, and hands engineers a pull request they can merge. The goal is simple: never learn the same expensive AI lesson twice.

Use it verbatim. Change it only after two prospects misunderstand the same thing.

## One line, when there is no room for four sentences

EverRule turns each AI-agent failure into a rule that stays meaningful even when the agent's execution path changes.

## Do not say

- "EverRule makes sure business rules survive however agents act." It overclaims runtime enforcement.
- "No one else does this." Recorders, gateways, policy engines and approval systems cover adjacent parts.
- "We replay the incident." Exact replay only where the evidence allows; otherwise a deterministic reproduction at the effect.
