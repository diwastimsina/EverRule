# Discovery kit, v4.2

Everything for selling while building, from doc 03 (Build + GTM plan). Nothing here is product.

## The decision

**November 15, 2026**, after 25 qualified conversations: at least 15 with operations owners and at least 5 with platform or developer-tooling leads.

| Outcome | Condition |
|---|---|
| Plan A continues | At least 5 companies report an enterprise-operations AI-agent incident with a stated dollar figure in the last 12 months, at least 3 name a realistic enforcement destination, and at least 1 commits to a paid pilot. |
| Plan B, chosen on purpose in writing with a revised offer | Plan A fails, at least 5 teams report a coding-agent incident with a stated cost in hours or dollars, and at least 1 will pay for a pilot. |
| Park | Both fail. Re-run 10 calls in nine months. |

Real incidents only. Scenarios and other-automation incidents never count. If someone will pay earlier in either segment, do not wait for call 25.

## Three counts, every call

1. Enterprise-operations AI-agent incidents
2. Coding-agent incidents
3. Other-automation incidents (RPA, cron, rule-based workflows). Recorded, never counted.

Ask all three, and the loss figure, in the first ten minutes, before the screen share. Demo-led calls produce feature requests instead of incident facts.

## Sequence

```text
demo works on the public URL
-> 5 early conversations
-> fix a major misunderstanding only
-> 20 more qualified conversations
-> paid design partner
```

## Files

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `pitch.md` | The locked four-sentence pitch. Verbatim in messages, call openings and the one-pager. |
| `lead-sources.md` | Where to find both segments. Qualify and disqualify rules. |
| `outreach.md` | Both messages, the split, the follow-up rule. |
| `discovery-script.md` | The call: facts first, demo second, close. Both segment variants. |
| `intake-template.md` | One copy per incident once a pilot starts. |
| `decision.md` | The November 15 worksheet. |
| `*.template.csv` | Committed empty sheets. Copy each to its working name before filling. |

Working sheets are local only and ignored by git, because they hold real names and quotes:

```
cp targets.template.csv targets.csv
cp discovery-log.template.csv discovery-log.csv
cp scorecard.template.csv scorecard.csv
```

## Weekly cadence at 12 hours

- 5 hours outreach and calls, taken first;
- 4 hours delivery or build, with what is left;
- 2 hours writing: proof reports, one short public post a month;
- 1 hour scorecard and planning.

If calls fall below three a week for two weeks, stop building until they recover.

## Money rule

Keep current income until two pilots are paid and one annual contract is committed.
