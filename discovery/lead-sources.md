# Where money-moving agents actually run

Ranked by expected hit rate. Likelihood is a guess until the log says otherwise.

1. **Support automation with refund or credit authority.** Companies using Sierra, Decagon, Intercom Fin, Zendesk AI agents, or Salesforce Agentforce at e-commerce, subscription SaaS, marketplaces, travel, and fintech. LinkedIn titles: Head of Support Operations, Refund, Customer Operations, Trust and Safety, at companies that have announced AI support deployments.
2. **Fintech operations.** Disputes, chargebacks, limit changes, account modifications. Titles: Payments Operations, Risk Operations, Fraud Operations.
3. **Internal IT and access provisioning.** ServiceNow and Okta workflows with AI actions. Titles: IT Operations, Identity, Access Governance.
4. **Procurement and accounts payable automation.** Coupa and SAP Ariba customers with AI copilots or AP agents. Titles: Procurement Operations, AP Manager, Controller.
5. **Platform teams that built in-house agents with tool access.** Find them through conference talks, engineering blog posts, published agent postmortems, and agent-framework communities such as LangChain and the OpenAI Agents SDK.
6. **Users of recorder and policy tools.** People starring, forking, or filing issues on AIR Blackbox, FlightBox, and the OPA repos already care about this problem.

## Warm paths

Ask every mentor and former colleague for two introductions to someone who owns refunds, payments operations, or an AI platform team. Ask QA and testing community contacts for introductions to platform teams.

## Qualify when

- the agent takes an action with a financial effect;
- a loss owner is identifiable;
- there is public evidence of the deployment.

## Disqualify when

- chat-only agents with no execution;
- no identifiable owner;
- the company is too small to have an operations owner separate from the founder.
