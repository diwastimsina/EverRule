## Redaction check before accepting

Refuse the export if any of these are present: customer names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, account numbers, card data, employee names beyond role, secrets or tokens.

Keep: amounts, timestamps, action names, tool names, decision fields, role names, approval types, model and prompt versions.
